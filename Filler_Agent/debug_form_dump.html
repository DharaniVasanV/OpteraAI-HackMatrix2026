All that you need to know about KONE Elevate'26

We are delighted to announce KONE Elevate'26, a National Level 30-Hour Hackathon organized by the Department of Artificial Intelligence and Data Science in association with KONE Elevator India Private Limited, Chennai. 

Event Details:

  • Event: KONE Elevate'26 – National Level Hackathon
  • Dates: 24th & 25th September 2026
  • Venue: Easwari Engineering College, Chennai
  • Prize Pool: ₹2,50,000

KONE Elevate'26 provides an exciting platform for students to:

  • Showcase their coding, analytical, and problem-solving skills.
  • Collaborate with talented innovators from diverse disciplines.
  • Develop impactful real-world solutions to industry-driven challenges.
  • Compete for attractive cash prizes, certificates, and national recognition.
